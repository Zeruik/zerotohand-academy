Modification and scope notice
Original triangle topology and material groups are retained with quantized coordinates and normals; visual data is bundled into JavaScript and the standalone HTML. UR5 xacro was expanded using upstream nominal UR5 parameters. These are kinematic teaching models, without collision, dynamics, balance, calibrated hardware control or vendor endorsement. Names identify asset provenance only. Full source details and hashes: assets/robots-v8/NOTICE.md and manifest.json in the source release.

Arduino CLI 1.5.1, Arduino-ESP32 3.3.10, Espressif compiler/SDK tools and esptool
5.3.0, together with required discovery, runtime and supporting components.
Only the selected supported targets and required runtime components are installed.
